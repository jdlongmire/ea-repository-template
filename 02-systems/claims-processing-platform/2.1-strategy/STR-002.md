# Claims Platform Consolidation Strategy

Consolidate three intake channels (web, phone-assisted, paper) onto one
platform with one intake interface, so a claim is a claim regardless of
the door it came through. Balance: migration cost is accepted in 2027
to retire two legacy stacks and their standing maintenance.
