---
id: REQ-002
type: requirement
name: Idempotent Claim Submission
level: systems
owner: enterprise-architecture
status: ratified
---

A retried claim submission never creates a duplicate claim. The
client supplies a claim token with each submission; the platform
deduplicates on it.

In the catalog (00-meta-model/catalog.toml) this requirement is
allocated to SYS-014 and realized through INT-003, whose artifact
carries the token mechanism.
