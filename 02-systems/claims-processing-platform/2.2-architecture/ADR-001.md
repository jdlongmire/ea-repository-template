# ADR-001: Event-Driven Integration for Claims Intake

Decision: intake components communicate through events on the enterprise
event backbone rather than point-to-point calls.

Context: three intake channels with different peak patterns; synchronous
coupling made the slowest channel everyone's outage.

Consequences: adjudication scales independently per channel; the team
accepts eventual consistency inside a claim's first hour and designs
status reporting around it.
