# DEC-002: Catalog, View Register, and Generated Views

Decision: the architecture model lives in a catalog
(00-meta-model/catalog.toml) of elements and typed relations. Every
catalog element is a typed artifact in this repository, bound by the
verifier (same id, type, name, and level), so catalog and artifacts
are two projections of one record. Views are registered in
00-meta-model/views.toml with a named viewpoint, a level, a focus
area, and membership and relation completeness flags, and they are
generated by 00-meta-model/render_views.py into 05-evidence/views/.
Generated views are never edited by hand; the verifier regenerates
them and fails on stale or unregistered outputs.

Context: typed headers say what each artifact is, but not how the
artifacts connect. Diagrams drawn outside the repository decayed
between review cycles and could not be checked against the record.
A picture of the architecture has to be a projection of the record,
or it becomes a second, weaker record.

Consequences: a new artifact type "requirement" joins schemas.json,
and the relation vocabulary is fixed there as data. Changing the
model means changing the catalog and regenerating; changing a view
means amending its register entry. Both arrive as pull requests and
are ratified like any other change.
