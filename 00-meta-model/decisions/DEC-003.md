# DEC-003: The Evidence Model

Decision: statements about the real estate arrive as evidence
artifacts (type: evidence) in 05-evidence/snapshots/, each carrying a
state kind, a subject, and provenance: source, method, observed_at,
and confidence. The state kinds are:

- **declared**: what a system or vendor says about itself.
- **observed**: what was directly measured or inspected.
- **inferred**: what was derived from other evidence.

The fourth state, the **authorized target**, is not an evidence
artifact. It is the record itself: the ratified artifacts in the
numbered baselines. Drift items (type: drift) in 05-evidence/drift/
record a mismatch between evidence and the authorized target and
must classify it: **defect** (the estate is wrong), **model-error**
(the record is wrong), **sensor-error** (the evidence is wrong), or
**undetermined** (the evidence cannot yet support a classification).
A drift item cites the evidence artifacts it rests on; the verifier
checks the citations.

Context: reconciliation compared modeled state to observed state and
treated the observation as ground truth. Observations are claims:
they arrive through registries, APIs, and telemetry that can be
incomplete, stale, or in conflict, as OBS-001 and OBS-002 are over
INT-007. An agent that cannot tell a direct measurement from a
vendor's self-report will file confident defects against healthy
systems and miss real ones.

Consequences: evidence without provenance no longer lands; the
verifier rejects it. Reconciliation becomes an act of weighing, and
its reasoning is part of the record. Changing the state kinds or the
classifications starts as an amendment to this decision.
