# DEC-001: Numbered Baselines and Typed Headers

Decision: the repository is organized in numbered baselines, the four
focus areas repeat as folders at every level, and every artifact
carries a schema-checked header.

Context: the previous architecture record lived in documents and a
tool database. It could not be diffed, reviewed as a change, or checked
by a machine, and it decayed between review cycles.

Consequences: structure is enforced by the verifier in CI, not by
convention. Any future change to the structure starts as an amendment
to this decision.
