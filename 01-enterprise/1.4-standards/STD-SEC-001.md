# Identity and Access for Systems

Every system authenticates users through the enterprise identity
provider and authorizes through named roles, never shared accounts.
Service-to-service calls use scoped, expiring credentials. Exceptions
are decided at ratification review and recorded as decisions in the
owning system's architecture folder. This standard is enforced by
review, not compiled policy, because its judgments are contextual.
