---
id: REQ-001
type: requirement
name: Claims Data Classified at Intake
level: enterprise
owner: enterprise-architecture
status: ratified
---

Every claim record carries a data classification from the moment of
intake, assigned under STD-DATA-002, and the classification travels
with the record through adjudication and payment.

In the catalog (00-meta-model/catalog.toml) this requirement is
allocated to CAP-001 and SYS-014; the Requirements Realization view
(VW-005) draws that allocation from the catalog.
