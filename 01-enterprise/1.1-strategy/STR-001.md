# Digital-First Service Strategy

Customers file, track, and settle claims through digital channels first.
Phone and paper remain supported, but no new capability is designed for
them first. Investment follows the capability map: claims intake and
processing is the first capability modernized under this strategy,
because it is where customer effort and operating cost both concentrate.
