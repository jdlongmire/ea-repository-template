# 01 Enterprise

Enterprise level: strategy and ecosystem alignment for the whole
estate. The four focus areas are folders, applied here as everywhere:

- `1.1-strategy`: where the enterprise is going.
- `1.2-architecture`: how the estate fits together (capabilities, target state).
- `1.3-roadmaps`: how the enterprise gets there, sequenced.
- `1.4-standards`: what governs decisions, including which standards
  are compiled to policy and which are enforced by review.
