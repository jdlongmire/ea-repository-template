# Claims Intake and Processing

The enterprise capability covering first notice of loss, evidence
collection, adjudication, and payment. Realized today by three intake
channels on separate stacks; the target state consolidates them onto the
Claims Processing Platform (SYS-014) with one intake interface (INT-003)
and one disbursement interface (INT-007).
